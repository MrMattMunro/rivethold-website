A trading hub at the crossing of four roads. A magitech experiment — built to power a new generation of airship — tore something open, and the Fall scattered everyone who survived.

Months later, settlers are trickling back. You and up to two friends arrive as strangers — a Woodcutter, a Scavenger, a Tinkerer — to help rebuild what's left and push back what came after.
